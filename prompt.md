I want to clone cronometer with a focus on redesigning the diary
behavior/experience to cater to advanced users who are looking to streamline
diet tracking. People who repeatedly eat the same meals have to spend the same
amount of time navigating through the app to add their meals. It ultimately
starts to feel tedious at some point.

Keep

- Food search, custom foods, and saved meals. Adjustable serving sizes and
- ingredient quantities. Calorie, macro, and available micronutrient tracking.
- Nutrition targets and editable logging history.

Change

- Make the main logging experience centered on one selected meal. Let users
- configure ordered sections and meal selections so their usual choices appear
- first. Keep logged entries alongside the current section. Offer optional
- progression between sections, rather than requiring repeated diary navigation.

Remove or leave out of scope

- Ads, subscriptions, and feature paywalls. Dashboard clutter and secondary
- features unrelated to routine logging. Cloud synchronization and external
- integrations for now.
